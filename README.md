

# ResearchVerse News Research Tool 📰🔍✨

Welcome to **ResearchVerse**, your go-to AI-powered assistant for news research!

***

## What Is It? 🤔

- ResearchVerse helps you make sense of news articles quickly and easily.
- Just enter up to 3 news article URLs and ask your question—get instant, concise answers with links to the sources.
- Useful for students, researchers, journalists, and anyone seeking verified info from current news.

***

## What Does It Do? 🚀

- 📝 Fetches and processes news articles directly from URLs you provide
- 📊 Breaks down long texts into manageable chunks and creates “smart” representations using AI
- 🧠 Understands your questions and finds relevant answers from your selected articles
- 🔗 Always cites source links, so you know exactly where info came from
- 🖥️ Super simple web interface with everything in one place

***

## Why Use It? 🌟

- Save time reading lengthy news stories—ask for key points or summaries
- Perfect for research projects, fact-checking, and staying informed
- Gives you control: analyze only the articles you choose
- No copy-pasting or manual searching needed



### Tech Stack 🚀

- 🐍 **Python** – The language powering everything behind the scenes.
- 🎨 **Streamlit** – Creates the interactive, web-based user interface.
- 🔗 **LangChain** – Connects large language models with your documents for smart Q&A.
- 🗝️ **OpenAI** – Provides advanced language capabilities for answering questions.
- 🧬 **FAISS** – Efficiently stores and searches “embeddings” for fast, accurate results.
- 🗄️ **pickle** – Saves and loads your article database locally.
- 📄 **unstructured** – Handles extraction of text from web news sources.
- 🛠️ Other helpers: **python-dotenv** for managing secret keys; **python-magic** and **libmagic** for smart file handling; **tiktoken** for token management.
